Our mission

Far more than a place to rest

As a not-for-profit, the Seamen's Club meets the physical, emotional and spiritual needs of everyone working at the Port. The goal: a welcoming environment that truly feels like home.

Crew members, business partners and visitors alike are warmly received. It is a port community where everyone feels included and supported.

Transport service

The shuttle that gets you off the pier

Port grounds are no place to walk. The Seamen's Club shuttle, driven by our volunteers, takes you from the ship to the club and into town: grocery store, pharmacy, shops and Old Québec.

Book at the front desk or by phone at 418 692-3376. Availability depends on the volunteers on duty.
